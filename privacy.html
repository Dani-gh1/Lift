Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version
  • Your workouts stay on your phone. We can't see them.
  • You don't need an account. We only store your email if you sign in to buy Premium or join the waitlist.
  • We count app usage anonymously – no cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites.
  • We never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

Daniel Hansen, Denmark (data controller).
Contact: danielsanchezhansen@gmail.com

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): sign-in, Premium and payment.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): the waitlist. You can withdraw it anytime.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): anonymous usage statistics to improve the app.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping payment records as required by Danish bookkeeping law.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase – sign-in and subscription database (EU region).
  • Stripe – payments. Stripe is responsible for its own processing of payment data; see stripe.com/privacy.
  • GoatCounter – privacy-friendly statistics.
  • Tally – waitlist form.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the app. Like any website host, it receives your IP address when you load the app.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr – deliver fonts and a code library. They receive your IP address when they are loaded.

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you delete it (Profile → Delete all my data) or clear your browser.
  • Sign-in and subscription data: as long as you have an account. Email us and we delete it.
  • Payment records: 5 years, as required by Danish bookkeeping law.
  • Waitlist emails: until Premium opens or you ask us to remove you.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Email danielsanchezhansen@gmail.com – we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, datatilsynet.dk.

7. Young users

LIFT is made for people aged 15 and up. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Buying Premium requires being 18 or having a parent's or guardian's permission.

8. Health data

Your workouts can say something about your health. That is one reason they stay on your device and are never sent to us.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes we tell signed-in users by email.
