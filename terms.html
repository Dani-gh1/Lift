LIFT is a web app with ready-made strength training programs, a workout log, a rest timer and progress charts. The free version includes the "Get Started" program, the basic log and backup. LIFT Premium adds all programs, tailoring to your equipment and goal, advanced stats and export to CSV/PDF.

Your workouts are stored on your own device. Please make a backup regularly (Profile → Save backup). We cannot restore data that was only stored on a lost or reset device.
